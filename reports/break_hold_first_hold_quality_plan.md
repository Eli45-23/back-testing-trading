# First Hold quality: fixed analysis plan

Declared before computing the new reference outcomes or feature-label comparisons, September 6, 2026.

- The immutable canonical population is 170 sessions, 1,435 breaks, 733 First Holds: 524 eventual-strong and 209 never-strong. Labels are joined only after feature construction. No break/hold detector is rerun.
- REFERENCE_CLOSE_V1 is read verbatim from the canonical JSON. FIRST_EXECUTABLE_MINUTE_OPEN_V1 uses the accepted Stage 9.2 selector: first validated same-session SPY SIP raw RTH minute starting at or after confirmation start + five minutes; reference is its open. The selected minute is included. Session-close entries are unavailable. This is an executable-timing reference, not a promise of filling at the historical open; latency/spread/slippage are not modeled.
- New excursions use the unchanged nine threshold pairs and EOD/pre-reclaim windows; fixed horizons run from the executable reference. Both legs of every comparison remain the same break identity. Missing entries remain counted and are excluded only from explicitly available-entry paired differences. Threshold denominators retain unavailable results.
- Features consume only a First Hold signal and a same-session prefix of completed five-minute bars. No outcome object, eventual label, later strong candle, or reclaim state is an input. Exact confirmation-row joins are required. Prefix-invariance tests will mutate/remove future bars.
- Reuse session-reset EMA9/20, Wilder ATR14, HLC3/volume VWAP, Stage 10.9 measurements, Stage 11.2 room selector, and Stage 11.3 confirmed-swing engine. Warm-up observations remain unavailable; no overnight carry or imputation. Room uses the frozen V1 known-level universe, not newly introduced levels. Missing previous/premarket levels remain explicitly flagged.
- Candle body is absolute close minus open; also retain signed direction-adjusted body. Close location is both raw (close−low)/range and direction-adjusted. Relative volume = confirmation volume / mean of the previous six completed same-session five-minute volumes, excluding confirmation; fewer than six prior bars is unavailable. Zero denominators are unavailable.
- Prior EMA-cross context uses the existing cross detector on the visible prefix. Same-close crosses are known; elapsed minutes are measured from cross completion. Opposite-boundary context uses strict breaks in completed post-opening candles; same-close and previously completed opposite breaks are distinct. Ranks reuse the frozen signal-time prior counts, plus one.
- Stage 11.1 requires an independently saved, causally available calibration artifact. If absent, regime labels are explicitly unavailable; do not recalculate retrospective boundaries. Their Stage 10.9 continuous inputs are still analyzed. This is a provenance limitation, not a zero value.
- Preserve every numeric feature continuously. Compare labels via n, availability, mean, median, Q25/Q75, and pooled-SD standardized mean difference (eventual minus never). Use all 170 whole-session clusters, 10,000 shared bootstrap draws, seed 20260907, percentile 95% intervals for mean differences. Zero-denominator resamples are counted, not silently used. Continuous outcome association uses Spearman correlation and point-biserial association to each of four predeclared clean-threshold indicators; ambiguity/no-data remain separate in accompanying outcome summaries. Primary threshold correlations use evaluable nonambiguous observations and disclose n.
- Categorical comparisons report counts, within-label percentages, category eventual-strong rate, both-reference outcome summaries at all four requested pairs, and pre-reclaim MFE/MAE. No profitability ranking. No new numeric buckets are required.
- Monthly comparisons are produced for **every** numeric and categorical feature, not just selected winners, and separately for LONG/SHORT. September is partial; n<30 or <10 sessions is flagged. No unrestricted feature combinations, classifier, score, filter, or exit optimization.
- All intervals are exploratory and pointwise, not multiplicity-adjusted confirmation. Correlated feature families and warm-up/time/direction confounding must temper any hypothesis. Selection of future hypotheses is descriptive only; all 2026 observations here remain discovery data.
- Preserve canonical source/config/report/data hashes; no broker connection, download, Stage 14 edit, commit or push.
