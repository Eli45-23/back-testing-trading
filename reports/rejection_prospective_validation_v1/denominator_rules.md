# Denominator rules

For each of the four frozen models and each cost/ambiguity sensitivity:

`eligible = executable + unavailable`

`executable = wins + losses + zero-R`

Ambiguous same-minute outcomes are a subset of executable, never an additional
denominator. ATR-unavailable signals remain in the ATR candidate's natural
eligible denominator and are not replaced by fixed stops. Zero-signal sessions are
retained in the fixed 60-session calendar and in whole-session bootstrap draws.

Natural populations are reported independently. Common-population tables may use
only exact entry identities present and usable in both compared populations; this
must never filter a natural candidate result. ATR common-population comparisons
use only exact identities where ATR is available, and disclose attrition.

Unavailable, ambiguous, and empty metrics carry explicit statuses/reasons. No
future-derived membership, level-family filter, directional filter, or month
removal is allowed.
